# Knowledge selection and budget
Save only information another engineer needs to continue or maintain the change: outcome, implementation locations, non-obvious why, constraints, tests, risks and next actions. Working memory (state/progress) is not long-term knowledge. T1: very short summary; decisions may be empty. T2: summarize affected interfaces and decisions. T3: include failure/rollback and data-security constraints when known. Deduplicate decisions and followups; avoid speculative lessons, routine command dumps and redundant project-wide documentation. Propose reusable ADRs, but require human decision before editing architecture or CLAUDE.md.
